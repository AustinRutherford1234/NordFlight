# Environment asset sources

Terrain packs were prepared November 3, 2026. Each pack manifest records its
source URLs, sample dimensions, vertical convention, hashes and byte counts.

- Copernicus DEM GLO-30 Public, 2021 release. Surface elevation source:
  https://registry.opendata.aws/copernicus-dem/
  License: https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
  Attribution for the adapted elevation data:
  produced using Copernicus WorldDEM-30 © DLR e.V. 2010-2014 and © Airbus Defence
  and Space GmbH 2014-2018 provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and
  ESA; all rights reserved.
  Adaptation: resampled, quantized, tiled height data; duplicate borders reconciled.
  These are DSM-derived game surfaces, not surveyed bare-earth terrain. Trees
  and buildings are not completely removed. Metre rounding is not metre accuracy.
- ESA WorldCover 2021 v200, CC BY 4.0:
  https://esa-worldcover.org/en/data-access
  © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data
  (2021) processed by ESA WorldCover consortium.
  Citation: Zanaga et al., 2022, ESA WorldCover 10 m 2021 v200,
  https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.7254221
  Adaptation: nearest-neighbor classifications resampled onto regional grids.
- NOAA ETOPO 2022 global relief, reduced to a 0.1-degree grid:
  https://www.ngdc.noaa.gov/mgg/global/relief/ETOPO2022/
  ETOPO 2022 60 arc-second surface elevation product. Public-domain US government
  data; source URL and dimensions are in packs/global/global.json.
- Poly Haven ground materials, CC0: https://polyhaven.com/license
  The material manifest lists every downloaded file, source and SHA-256.
  Adaptation: contrast normalization, resizing, mipmaps, texture arrays, BC3.
  Retained JPEGs are source inputs, not additional runtime texture allocations.
- Existing NASA Earth imagery retains its attribution in ../EARTH_SOURCE.md.

The terrain importer interprets source mean-sea-level heights as radial offsets
from the game's spherical reference radius. It does not turn the simulator into
an ellipsoidal/geoid survey system. WorldCover dates are historical, not current
vegetation or building inventories. Procedural trees and settlement blocks are
representative detail, not measured individual objects.

Vegetation silhouettes and primitive rocks/buildings are generated by project
code. There are no third-party model licenses for these meshes.

`menu-earth.jpg` is a 2048 × 1024 derivative of the existing NASA `earth.jpg`,
used only by the fixed-size menu planet preview. Flight/orbital imagery retains
its original resolution. See ../EARTH_SOURCE.md for the original attribution.
