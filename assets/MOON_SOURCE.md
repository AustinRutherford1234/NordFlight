# Lunar color mosaic

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio; Ernie Wright (USRA), with LROC/LOLA instrument-team source data.

- Source page: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
- Source image: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_16bit_srgb_4k.tif
- Product: December 2025 lunar color map, 4096 × 2048, centered on 0° longitude.
- Local asset: `moon.jpg`, converted from the sRGB TIFF to JPEG quality 92 for distribution size. No geographic edits.
- Acquired October 16, 2026.

This is an appearance texture. The simulator retains its procedural lunar contact terrain and does not claim surveyed landing-site topography. The texture maps body −Z to north and body −Y to the Earth-facing prime meridian in the prescribed synchronous lunar model.
